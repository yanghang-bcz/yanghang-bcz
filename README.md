I’m a student in Shanghai. This is where I keep the things I’m making and the questions that started them.

Learning, judgment, economics. A few small web experiments, still in progress.

## side a / tracklist

### 01 · Truth Detective

A browser investigation game about what evidence can actually support. Explore a 3D case, weigh different sources, and revisit your first judgment—with an AI analyst limited to the evidence you’ve found.

**[Try it! ↗](https://truth-detective-07655.app.workbuddy.host/)** &nbsp;·&nbsp; [Source code](https://github.com/yanghang-bcz/truth-detective)

### 02 · CalcDaily

Calculus practice for the postgraduate entrance exam. Difficulty adjusts as you work; weak topics return for another pass. Generated questions go through mathematical checks, with verified fallback problems when needed.

**[Try it! ↗](https://calcdaily.cn/)** &nbsp;·&nbsp; [Source code](https://github.com/yanghang-bcz/math-practice-demo)

### 03 · Campus Pulse

A research prototype for making sense of campus feedback. Related comments become traceable signals, with the original feedback still within reach and people keeping the final say. The public demo is read-only and uses synthetic data.

**[Try it! ↗](https://campus-pulse.app.workbuddy.host/)** &nbsp;·&nbsp; [Source code](https://github.com/yanghang-bcz/campus-pulse)

### 04 · FinModel Lab

Financial models you can move around. Change a parameter, follow the chart, and connect the math to a complete R script you can run yourself. A workbench for building intuition about risk, portfolios, and pricing.

**[Try it! ↗](https://finmodel-lab.app.workbuddy.host/?sharecode=PLWKCGJYNW)** &nbsp;·&nbsp; [Source code](https://github.com/yanghang-bcz/finmodel-lab)
